The 5-1-1 guide

The usual signal to call is contractions about five minutes apart, each lasting around one minute, keeping that up for one hour. For a first baby most clinics want you to wait for that pattern rather than coming in at the first twinge.

Call straight away whatever the timer says if her waters break, if there is bleeding, if the pain is constant rather than coming in waves, or if the baby's movement drops off.
