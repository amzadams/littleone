The car seat

The fire station thing is a myth. Being a firefighter, nurse, doctor or police officer does not come with any car seat training. The only people qualified are certified Child Passenger Safety Technicians.

Worth doing properly. Safe Kids Grand Forks says four out of five car seats are used incorrectly, and it is worst with the complicated infant seats for newborns.

Three things to get right

Book it around week 36
Before the baby comes, not after. The hospital will not discharge you without a seat fitted.
Buy new, never second hand
You cannot see crash damage, and you will not know if it has been recalled.
Rear facing, for a long time
Moving a child forward too early is one of the most common mistakes. North Dakota law requires a restraint until age 8.

Worth looking into

Pregnancy Medicaid
The unborn baby counts in the household size, which lifts the income limit. No asset test. Apply at HealthCare.gov or Grand Forks County Social Services.
WIC
Food support during pregnancy and after. Separate from Medicaid, with a higher income limit, so worth applying even if Medicaid does not come through.
Breast pump through insurance
Usually covered. Ask before buying one.
Hospital tour and birth classes
Altru runs both. Ask at the next visit, they book up.
