How this works

From around week 28, pick a time of day when she is usually active, often after a meal or in the evening, and count until ten movements. Kicks, rolls and jabs all count, hiccups do not. Ten inside two hours is the usual reassurance.

What matters more than any number is a change from her own normal. If movement drops off clearly, call the clinic the same day. Do not wait and see, and do not try cold drinks or sugar to get the baby moving first.
