Settings

Due date

Change this to whatever the clinic gives after the scan. Everything else updates from it.

Work out the due date

If you only know the first day of her last period, put it here.

Notifications

Lets the site pop a message when the other person posts an update. On iPhone, add the site to the home screen first or this will not work.

Add to home screen

On iPhone, open this page in Safari, tap the share button, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open the browser menu and tap Install app. It then opens like a normal app.
